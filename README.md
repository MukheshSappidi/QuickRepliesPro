# ⚡ Quick Replies Pro

A blazing-fast, lightweight open-source desktop quick-reply utility built for power users, customer support agents, and professionals using WhatsApp Business, CRM systems, or any text-heavy application on Windows.

---

## 🎯 Who Is This For?

QuickRepliesPro is designed for anyone who types the same messages over and over again throughout the day:
- **Customer Support Teams**: Instantly reply to common customer queries across WhatsApp, Zendesk live chats, support tickets, and helpdesk emails (Gmail, Outlook) without typing everything manually.
- **Sales & Marketing Professionals**: Quickly drop pitch links, pricing sheets, and follow-up templates into emails, client chats, or CRMs.
- **Freelancers & Community Managers**: Save hours of repetitive typing when handling client emails, support inquiries, or moderation queries.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Dual Global Hotkey Triggers**: Instantly invoke your search popup anywhere using **Alt + Q** or the **backtick key** use this ` located right above your Tab key (ideal for standard QWERTY layouts).
- **Zero Key Conflicts**: Engineered to completely bypass native app shortcut restrictions.
- **Smart Real-Time Filtering**: Type a keyword to instantly filter through your custom templates.
- **Lightning-Fast Auto-Paste Engine**: Automatically copies your selected template to the system clipboard and simulates Ctrl + V to drop the text seamlessly into your active window.
- **Persistent SQLite Backend**: Local data storage with full usage tracking.
- **JSON Portability**: Easily import or export your template library for backups or sharing.
- **System Tray Integration**: Runs quietly in your Windows system tray without taskbar clutter.

---

## 🧭 How to Use Quick Replies Pro

1. **Trigger the Popup**: Press **Alt + Q** or the **backtick key** use this ` (right above Tab) from any app (WhatsApp, Gmail, Notepad, browser, etc.).
2. **Search / Filter**: Type a few characters of your keyword to narrow down your templates in real-time.
3. **Navigate with Arrows**: Use your keyboard's **Up and Down arrow keys** to highlight the reply you want from the list.
4. **Auto-Paste**: Press **Enter** on your keyboard. The app will automatically copy the text and paste it (Ctrl + V) directly into your active chat or email window!
